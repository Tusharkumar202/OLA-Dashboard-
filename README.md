# 🚗 OLA Ride Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

An interactive **OLA Ride Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI** to analyze ride bookings, revenue, vehicle performance, cancellations, customer behavior, and ratings.

The dashboard provides a complete view of ride operations through multiple analytical pages, helping identify important trends and performance patterns across different vehicle types and ride categories.

## 🔍 Key Features

* 🚕 **Overall Performance** – Track total rides, successful bookings, booking status, and ride volume over time.
* 🚗 **Vehicle Type Analysis** – Compare bookings, successful rides, distance traveled, and performance across different vehicle categories.
* 💰 **Revenue Analysis** – Analyze revenue by payment method, booking value, customer contribution, and daily booking trends.
* ❌ **Cancellation Analysis** – Identify cancellation rates and understand the major reasons for cancellations by drivers and customers.
* ⭐ **Rating Analysis** – Compare driver and customer ratings across different vehicle types.
* 📅 **Date Filters** – Analyze ride performance for specific dates and time periods.
* 📊 **Interactive Visualizations** – KPI cards, donut charts, bar charts, trend analysis, and dynamic filters.

## 🛠️ Tools & Skills

**Microsoft Power BI | DAX | Data Cleaning | Data Transformation | Data Visualization | Business Intelligence | Dashboard Design**

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project was to build a comprehensive ride analytics solution that transforms raw booking data into actionable business insights and helps understand **ride demand, revenue performance, vehicle utilization, cancellations, and customer experience**.

## 💡 Business Insights

The dashboard can help analyze:

* Overall booking and ride performance
* Successful vs. cancelled bookings
* Revenue and payment method trends
* Vehicle-wise booking performance
* Ride distance and volume
* Driver and customer cancellation reasons
* Driver and customer ratings
* Daily ride and booking trends
* Customer contribution to revenue
